Anki Clicker controls

What each button does during a review. Hold the Joy-Con (L) sideways, with the stick on your left.

Button positions follow how Anki reads a sideways Joy-Con. If a label doesn't match what a button does, tell Azeem which one.

Can't grade by accident

On the question side, any grading button just shows the answer. It grades only after you've seen it.

Undo works like Ctrl+Z

Minus takes back your last answer. The stats skip anything you undo.

Scrolling needs a long card

A light push creeps, a full push moves fast. On short cards the stick does nothing, which is normal.
